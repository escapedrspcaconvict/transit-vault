Where is my data stored?
Your data is stored strictly on this device's local storage. No backends or external servers are used. It is E2E encrypted if 2FA is enabled.
What happens if I forget my 2FA Passcode?
There is absolutely no recovery. The passcode acts as your unique encryption key. If lost, your vault data is permanently unreadable.
How do I interact with the Vault?
Tap any entry once to copy the data to your clipboard. Press and hold an entry for 1.5 seconds to drag and reorder the list.
How do API Keys work?
To fetch live bus/train data, you must provide your own API keys. We encrypt them locally, and they are only decrypted at the exact moment a fetch request is made directly from your browser.
